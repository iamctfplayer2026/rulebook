    • What Do I Need to Bring?

    • Your NFSU Student ID (mandatory for entry and eligibility verification).
    • A laptop capable of connecting to the lab network (wired or wireless). Lab systems will also be available.
    • Your registration confirmation (emailed after you register). If you didn't receive one, contact a coordinator immediately.
    • Your thinking cap and your sense of humor. This is a celebration of Independence Day as much as it is a competition.

    Competition Rules

    1. Don't be a jerk. Attacking fellow participants, organizers, or competition infrastructure outside the defined challenge scope will result in immediate disqualification. Only interact with listed challenge targets.
    2. No Denial of Service (DoS), disruption, or platform abuse. No large-scale bruteforcing, automated scanning, or actions that degrade the platform unless explicitly required by a challenge.
    3. Solo competition only. No teaming, sharing flags, solutions, or methodologies during the event.
    4. Eligibility. Only currently enrolled NFSU students may compete. Student ID verification may occur at any time.
    5. No cheating or unauthorized external help. Solve challenges using your own skills. Do not search for or share exact flag answers online.
    6. Stay in scope. Do not attack systems outside the designated CTF environment, including the scoreboard, campus infrastructure, or unrelated services.
    7. Flag format. Every valid flag follows the format SWARAJ{...}.
    8. No brute-forcing flags. Excessive incorrect submissions may trigger cooldowns, penalties, or suspension.
    9. Tie-breaking. If scores are equal, the participant who reached the score first (earlier last-solve timestamp) ranks higher. (Confirm with your scoring platform before publishing.)
    10. Only approved tools. Standard public cybersecurity tools are allowed. Tools intended to disrupt the competition are prohibited.
    11. Illegal or reckless behavior may result in disqualification and referral to NFSU disciplinary processes where applicable.
    12. Respect physical boundaries. Maintain appropriate conduct within the computer labs.
    13. No offensive or abusive language. This includes chat messages, usernames, aliases, or interactions.
    14. No physical or emotional abuse. Harassment, humiliation, ridicule, or degrading behavior toward anyone is prohibited.
    15. Organizer's decision is final. Harshil, Dhruvil, and Om have the final authority on disputes and rule interpretation.
    16. Rule violations may result in immediate disqualification, forfeiture of prizes, and removal from the competition.
