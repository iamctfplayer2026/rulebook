CTF Rules & Guidelines

National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU)

Competition Rules

  1. No Denial of Service (DoS), disruption, or platform abuse. No large-scale bruteforcing, automated scanning, or actions that degrade the platform unless explicitly required by a challenge.
  2. Solo competition only. No teaming, sharing flags, solutions, or methodologies during the event.
  3. Eligibility. Only currently enrolled NFSU students may compete. Student ID verification may occur at any time.
  4. No cheating or unauthorized external help. Solve challenges using your own skills. Do not search for or share exact flag answers online.
  5. Stay in scope. Do not attack systems outside the designated CTF environment, including the scoreboard, campus infrastructure, or unrelated services.
  6. Flag format. Every valid flag follows the format Swaraj{...}.
  7. No brute-forcing flags. Excessive incorrect submissions may trigger cooldowns, penalties, or suspension.
  8. Tie-breaking. If scores are equal, the participant who reached the score first (earlier last-solve timestamp) ranks higher.
  9. Only approved tools. Standard public cybersecurity tools are allowed. Tools intended to disrupt the competition are prohibited.
  10. Internet connectivity. The college/organizers do not guarantee the availability, reliability, speed, or uninterrupted access to the internet during the competition. Participants are responsible for arranging and managing their own internet connection if required for the CTF.
  11. Illegal or reckless behavior may result in disqualification and referral to NFSU disciplinary processes where applicable.
  12. Respect physical boundaries. Maintain appropriate conduct within the computer labs.
  13. No offensive or abusive language. This includes chat messages, usernames, aliases, or interactions.
  14. No physical or emotional abuse. Harassment, humiliation, ridicule, or degrading behavior toward anyone is prohibited.
  15. Organizer's decision is final. Harshil, Dhruvil, and Om have the final authority on disputes and rule interpretation.
  16. Rule violations may result in immediate disqualification, forfeiture of prizes, and removal from the competition.
